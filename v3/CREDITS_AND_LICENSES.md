## Visual & Audio Assets

- **3D Geometry**: 100% procedural Three.js geometry (Islamic arches, stars, courtyard lattice, stone plinth, and wax egg generated via native Three.js classes and SVG path coordinates). Original code with zero third-party 3D models or stock imagery.
- **Logos**:
  - GMSA-KNUST Association Logo: Custom vector emblem provided by GMSA-KNUST.
  - IT & Media Committee Logo: Custom vector emblem created for the GMSA-KNUST IT & Media Committee.
- **Audio & Ambience**: Synthesized procedural soft glass tick and natural ambience placeholders. No commercial music, copyrighted performances, or licensed sound recordings.
